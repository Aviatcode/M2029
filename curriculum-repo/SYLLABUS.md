## 📘 Database Management Systems (BCA-302)
* **Credits:** 4.0 Credits
* **Faculty In-Charge:** Dr. Neha Verma (faculty@cs.university.edu)
* **Designated Lecture Room:** `Room 402 / Lab 3`
* **Prescribed Textbook:** *Database System Concepts (7th Edition) by Silberschatz, Korth, Sudarshan (McGraw-Hill)*

### Units & Module Breakdown:

#### 📌 Unit 1: Relational Model & ER Design
* **Allocated Teaching Hours:** 6 Hours
* **University Exam Weightage:** `15 - 25 Marks`

**Curriculum Topics & Competencies:**
- [ ] **Entity-Relationship (ER) Diagrams & Weak Entities** (6 mins study) ⭐ **[10-MARKER UNIVERSITY EXAM TOPIC]**
- [ ] **Relational Algebra: Select, Project, Join & Set Operations** (7 mins study)

#### 📌 Unit 2: Normalization & Functional Dependencies
* **Allocated Teaching Hours:** 6 Hours
* **University Exam Weightage:** `15 - 25 Marks`

**Curriculum Topics & Competencies:**
- [ ] **Functional Dependencies & Closure of Attribute Sets** (5 mins study) ⭐ **[10-MARKER UNIVERSITY EXAM TOPIC]**
- [ ] **Normalization: 1NF, 2NF, 3NF & Boyce-Codd (BCNF)** (8 mins study) ⭐ **[10-MARKER UNIVERSITY EXAM TOPIC]**

#### 📌 Unit 3: Transaction Processing & Concurrency
* **Allocated Teaching Hours:** 6 Hours
* **University Exam Weightage:** `15 - 25 Marks`

**Curriculum Topics & Competencies:**
- [ ] **ACID Properties & Transaction State Diagram** (5 mins study)
- [ ] **Two-Phase Locking (2PL) Protocol & Serializability** (6 mins study) ⭐ **[10-MARKER UNIVERSITY EXAM TOPIC]**
