# 📖 Comprehensive Syllabus: Database Management Systems & Relational Architectures (BCA-302)

**Course:** Database Management Systems & Relational Architectures  
**Code:** BCA-302  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science & Applications  
**University:** University School of Information Technology & Engineering  
**Instructor:** Dr. Rajesh Sharma (rajesh.sharma@cs.university.edu)  
**Credits:** 4.0 Credits (3L + 1T)  

---

## Unit 1: Data Modeling and Conceptual Database Architecture
* **Weightage:** 15 Marks
* **Allocated Hours:** 10 Hours

### Topics:
1. **Introduction to Database Systems, Schema vs Instance, Three-Schema Architecture**
2. **Entity-Relationship (ER) Modeling: Entities, Relationships, Multiplicity Constraints, Weak Entities**
3. **Enhanced ER (EER) modeling, Generalization, Specialization, Categorization**

---

## Unit 2: The Relational Model and Relational Languages
* **Weightage:** 20 Marks
* **Allocated Hours:** 11 Hours

### Topics:
1. **Relational data structure, integrity constraints: domain, key, entity, and referential integrity**
2. **Relational Algebra: Selection, Projection, Set operations, Cartesian product, Joins, Division**
3. **Relational Calculus: Tuple Relational Calculus (TRC) and Domain Relational Calculus (DRC)**
4. **SQL DDL, DML, DCL, Views, Complex Nested Subqueries, Aggregate Operators**

---

## Unit 3: Relational Database Design and Normalization
* **Weightage:** 25 Marks
* **Allocated Hours:** 12 Hours

### Topics:
1. **Informal design guidelines for relation schemas, Anomalies in schema design**
2. **Functional Dependencies (FDs), Closure of attribute sets, Canonical cover, Armstrong Axioms**
3. **Normal Forms based on primary keys: 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF)**
4. **Multi-valued dependencies and 4NF, Join dependencies and 5NF, Lossless join and Dependency preservation**

---

## Unit 4: Transaction Processing, Concurrency, and Recovery
* **Weightage:** 20 Marks
* **Allocated Hours:** 10 Hours

### Topics:
1. **Transaction concept, ACID properties, Transaction states, Concurrent executions**
2. **Serializability: Conflict and View serializability, Precedence Graph**
3. **Concurrency Control: Two-Phase Locking (2PL), Strict 2PL, Timestamp ordering, Deadlocks**
4. **Crash Recovery: Log-based recovery, Immediate vs Deferred update, Checkpoints, ARIES algorithm**

---

## Prescribed References
1. *Database System Concepts* by Abraham Silberschatz, Henry F. Korth, S. Sudarshan (McGraw-Hill Education, 7th Edition)
2. *Fundamentals of Database Systems* by Ramez Elmasri, Shamkant B. Navathe (Pearson Education, 8th Edition)
